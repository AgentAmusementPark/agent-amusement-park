What the name means

Is A2AParkBench available?

Its public website, free regression runner, and fixed public corpus are available. Private hosted history, comparison, customer entitlement, paid CI, and immediate paid fulfilment remain gated.

Is it only for protocol-native A2A agents?

No. A2AParkBench covers browser and A2A agents; “A2A” is part of the A2APark brand, not a protocol-only restriction.

Is this a safety certification?

No. A2APark produces behavioral evidence from explicit simulated worlds. It does not certify an agent as safe.
