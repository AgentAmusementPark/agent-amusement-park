What carries over?

Can I claim a Park scorecard in Bench?

No. The Park scorecard is a signed summary of one run. Bench currently has no Park scorecard import or claim flow.

Do the two scores compare directly?

No. Park rides and Bench regression cases are separate test surfaces with different evidence.

Is this a safety certification?

No. Both products report behavior in defined tests; neither certifies general safety.
