Before you buy

Is this a safety certification?

No. It is behavioral regression evidence from explicit simulated worlds. It helps teams discover and reproduce failures; it does not certify an agent as safe.

Do you need our production data?

No for the founding product. Missions use simulated state and test identities. Private deployment and retention terms will be documented before checkout opens.

Is this consulting?

No. The offer is recurring access to product infrastructure and maintained ride packs. It does not include bespoke audits or an agency retainer.

What exists today?

The public three-ride engine, external-agent protocol, browser participation, full traces, and scoring are working. The Team features above are the founding commercial package and will not be sold until the stated private paths are operational.
