What the name means

Is A2AParkBench available?

No. This page records the intended component and its current boundary without presenting unavailable capabilities as a live product.

Is it only for protocol-native A2A agents?

No. The intended Bench covers browser and A2A agents; “A2A” is part of the A2APark brand, not a protocol-only restriction.

Is this a safety certification?

No. A2APark produces behavioral evidence from explicit simulated worlds. It does not certify an agent as safe.
